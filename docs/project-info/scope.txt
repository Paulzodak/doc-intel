Our Legal Document Analyzer transforms the tedious process of reviewing lengthy legal documents into a fast, accurate, and intuitive experience. Leveraging AI, the platform allows users to quickly analyze any legal document, providing a clear, actionable summary that highlights key points, identifies potential risks, and evaluates advantages or benefits. Additionally, the system grades documents across multiple metrics such as risk, compliance with local standards, and overall legal quality.
Key Features
1. Document Upload
Multiple Formats Supported: Upload documents in PDF, DOCX, MD, ODT, HTML, EPUB, RTF, or TXT formats.


Document Scanning: Scan physical documents via camera or image selection for instant analysis.


Direct Text Input: Paste your document directly into a built-in text editor for quick processing.


Batch Upload (Optional Enhancement): Upload multiple documents at once for streamlined analysis.


2. Document Analysis
Key Points Extraction: Automatically identifies and lists crucial legal points.


Risk Assessment: Detects potential risks, liabilities, and compliance gaps.


Advantages & Benefits Analysis: Highlights key benefits or opportunities present in the document.


Grading & Metrics: Scores documents on risk, advantages, compliance, and potential issues.


AI Chat Integration: Interact with your document via AI to explore additional insights, ask questions, or add custom grading metrics.


Clause-Level Analysis (Suggested Enhancement): Evaluate individual clauses for risk, compliance, and alignment with best practices. (V2)


Compliance Recommendations (Suggested Enhancement): AI suggests corrective actions or improvements to meet local legal standards. (V2)


3. Result Management
Export Options: Save results as PDF or high-quality image for sharing or record-keeping.


Temporary Shareable Links: Generate secure links to share document analysis with others.


Version History (Suggested Enhancement): Track changes or updates in documents and re-analyze older versions.


Highlighting & Annotations (Suggested Enhancement): Visual highlights of risks, advantages, and compliance issues within the document.


4. Security & Privacy (Suggested Enhancement)
Data Privacy: All uploaded documents are encrypted and securely processed.


Temporary Storage: Documents and results are automatically deleted after a set period to ensure confidentiality.


Access Controls: Option to password-protect shared links or restrict access.

Make Informed Decisions: Understand the advantages, liabilities, and key points at a glance.
